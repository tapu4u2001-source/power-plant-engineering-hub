Maintenance Fundamentals

Insulation Resistance & PI Testing

  • Megger testing
  • IR measurement
  • Polarization Index
  • Temperature effect on insulation resistance
  • Phase-to-earth and phase-to-phase checks

Lesson 11

Protection Relay Trip Investigation

Never troubleshoot a trip by resetting blindly. First identify what protection operated and why.

  • Record relay target / indication
  • Check event record where available
  • Identify trip command path
  • Check field equipment condition
  • Verify DC control supply
  • Reset only after investigation and authorization
Lesson 12

  • Fuse / MCB condition

  • Control circuit voltage

Lesson 16

Condition-Based Maintenance

Maintenance can be planned from equipment condition and trends rather than relying only on fixed intervals.

  • Temperature trends

Lesson 17

Root-Cause Analysis

The objective is not merely to restore the equipment. The objective is to understand why the failure occurred.

  • Define the problem
  • Collect evidence
  • Separate symptoms from causes
  • Identify contributing factors
  • Determine corrective action
  • Verify recurrence prevention
Lesson 18

5-Why Troubleshooting Method

Ask why repeatedly until the investigation reaches a controllable underlying cause rather than stopping at the symptom.

  • Start with the actual failure
  • Use evidence for every answer
  • Avoid assumptions
  • Check contributing conditions
  • Document the final cause
Lesson 19

Practical Trip Analysis

A plant trip should be reconstructed as a sequence of events.

  • What happened first?
  • Which alarm appeared first?
  • Which protection operated?
  • Which breaker changed state?
  • What equipment condition existed?
  • What evidence supports the conclusion?
Symptom → Evidence → Cause → Corrective Action → Verification
Lesson 20

Case Study: Motor Incomer Tripping

A repeated motor start/stop problem should be investigated from both the electrical and process sides.

  • Check start frequency and operating sequence
  • Check starting current
  • Check overload settings
  • Check motor and cable insulation
  • Check mechanical load
  • Check starter transition and control logic
Lesson 21

Case Study: Transformer Alarm / Trip

Transformer investigations should begin with the exact protection or alarm indication.

  • Record alarm / trip indication
  • Check loading
  • Check temperature
  • Check cooling system
  • Check oil level and visible condition
  • Review protection event information
Lesson 22

Maintenance Safety & Isolation

Safe isolation must come before maintenance activity. Follow the plant's approved procedures and permits.

  • Identify the correct equipment
  • Isolate all relevant energy sources
  • Apply approved lockout/tagout procedures
  • Verify absence of voltage where applicable
  • Use appropriate PPE
  • Restore equipment only through the approved procedure

🧰 Maintenance Engineer Checklist

  • Understand the equipment and its normal operating condition.
  • Record the actual symptom before resetting or dismantling.
  • Check alarms, trips and protection indications.
  • Check electrical supply and control supply.
  • Inspect physical condition.
  • Measure before replacing components whenever practical.
  • Compare readings with previous healthy-condition data.
  • Separate the immediate symptom from the root cause.
  • Document the failure, action taken and final cause.
  • Verify equipment condition before returning it to service.

Engineering principle: Don't troubleshoot by guesswork. Follow the evidence.
